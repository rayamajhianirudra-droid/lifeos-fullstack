Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026 · LifeOS Health (www.lifeoshealth.com)

LifeOS Health is a nutrition and fitness tracker built and run by Anirudra Rayamajhi in Minnesota, USA ("we"). This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the app, and how you can download or delete your data. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising cookies.

Weight, food, sleep, mood and similar entries are health data. We only collect them because you enter them, and you agree to this when you create an account. You can take that consent back at any time by deleting your account.

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. If you use LifeOS from another country, your data is transferred to the US.

Stored in your browser

We keep your sign-in token, a copy of your profile, your name and your dark mode setting in your browser's local storage so the app works between visits. Signing out removes them. We do not use cookies for tracking.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your account and every log, custom food, plan and reminder are permanently removed from our database at once. Your sessions on all devices stop working. Backups kept by our database provider expire on their own within days.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Profile, then Your data, then Download my data gives you everything we store about you as a file.
  • Delete your account: Profile, then Your data, then Delete account. You confirm with your password.
  • Correct your data: edit your profile or any log in the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have more rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your data protection authority. Email us and we will help.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sign-in attempts and other requests are rate limited, and each account can only read its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

LifeOS is not meant for children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to sign up. If you are under 16 and live in the EU or UK, ask a parent or guardian before using it. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Not medical advice

Calorie goals, AI coach answers and other suggestions are general information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before making big changes to your diet or exercise, especially if you have a health condition.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
